# Assumptions & Limitations Log

Deliverable 4. Curated for reviewers; grouped into four sections. `Type` is one of Decision, Assumption, Limitation, Data gap. Each row is marked **[verified]** (checked against the data or files, reproducible by the named script or `key_numbers.csv` id), **[assumed]**, **[inferred]** (a reading of verified facts that has not been confirmed) or **[unverified]**. Rows that record a project decision rather than a data claim say so. Where several entries covered the same item they are merged, and the merged row notes the earlier entries and their dates. Superseded values are kept in the merged row, labelled as such.

Reading guide: the numbers behind the four final insights (I1 to I4) are the `f_` rows in `reports/insights/key_numbers.csv`. Fault types and values have the meanings given in the brief only; any code, unit or field not stated in the brief or the two bin JSONs is "unknown" and is listed under Additional data needed.

Sections: 1. Data limitations - 2. Assumptions (and the decisions that fix them) - 3. Additional data needed - 4. Known gaps - 5 to 7. Insight rounds (13 Oct) - 8. Final tidy. Rows marked **Superseded** are kept for the audit trail; the final insights (I1 to I4, section 7) take precedence over first-round findings 1 to 3.
