# Step 6: EDA shortlist (exploratory)

Generated by `notebooks/01_eda_shortlist.ipynb` (helpers in `src/eda_helpers.py`); reads only `data/model/`. Unit of analysis: the machine. Differences are Return/Repair (RR) minus Normal on machine-level medians (shares for "% machines"). Intervals: bootstrap 95% of the difference (machines resampled within cohort, 2000 resamples, seed 20261011). **All results are exploratory**: several comparisons are made, so no single interval is proof. RR is a selected population. Nothing here explains causes; each "so what" is a hypothesis.

"Direction holds" = the variant gives the same sign of the RR - Normal difference as the main result (yes / no / n/a when a difference is zero). Each entry shows Normal vs RR values and n machines.

## Final selection

Gate decision (user), recorded 2026-10-11. All results remain exploratory; Return/Repair (RR) is a selected population; no causes are claimed.

**Method rule.** Fault rates are **pooled** (total faults / total sessions x 100), with bootstrap 95% intervals from resampling machines (2,000 resamples, fixed seed), because most machines have zero for rare fault types. Continuous measures (motor runtime per day, durations) use **machine-level medians** with the same bootstrap.

**Final insights for Step 7**

1. **Fault counts are driven by model, mainly Filter Wash (13/1); cohort comparisons must be like-for-like (SV53).** Within Normal, pooled faults per 100 sessions are 16.5 (SV50, 99 machines) vs 4.3 (SV53, 59 machines); Filter Wash is 11.4 vs 1.5 and makes up 81.4% of the gap. Excluding 13/1 the gap is -2.3 (interval -5.3 to 0.7). Filter Wash is treated as a maintenance alert; this grouping is ours, not the brief's. Step 7 chart: side-by-side fault rate with and without 13/1.
2. **Among SV53 machines, RR raise airway blockage at several times the Normal rate, including per hour of motor runtime.** Type 12 (values 1+2), pooled per 100 sessions: Normal 2.8 vs RR 13.1 (4.7x; interval of the difference 1.7 to 24.7; 59 / 36 machines). 12/2 alone (stricter): 1.1 vs 6.5 (5.9x; 1.5 to 11.5). Per motor runtime hour (45 / 30 machines): type 12 0.32 vs 1.73 (5.4x; 0.17 to 3.12); 12/2 alone 0.09 vs 0.87 (0.23 to 1.51). Direction held under ROW-PROD only and SV53-AB only; the SV53-AB type 12 interval spans 0, while 12/2 alone excludes 0.
3. **RR machines run about twice the motor minutes per day.** Median per machine 2.38 (Normal, 54 machines) vs 4.87 (RR, 36): 2.04x, difference 2.5 min/day (0.43 to 3.98). Direction held for ROW-PROD only, over common calendar months (both versions) and SV53-AB only (interval spans 0, 15 / 22 machines). Observation windows differ (valid motor days: median 240 Normal vs 91 RR).

**Dropped or caveat-only:** C3 motor load (no difference; driver of the band not stated), C5a battery used (coarse, interval touches 0), C5b battery health (median 99 in both), C6 end-of-record fault change (Normal also rises; RR - Normal not distinguishable), and the 12/1 and 13/1 share results (direction flips under SV53-only).
