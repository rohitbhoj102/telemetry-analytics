Generated by `src/make_deck_numbers.py` from the `f_` rows of `reports/insights/key_numbers.csv`; do not edit by hand. ## Do NOT put on slides (not produced by a script)

These came from exploratory queries that are not in the repository scripts, so they are not in `key_numbers.csv` and cannot be reproduced; keep them off the slides.

- Motor profile vs machine blockage rate: 'no motor feature has |rho| above 0.3' (exploratory correlations on 95 SV53 machines).
- `filter_wash_counter`: 'rises about 8.6 per runtime hour in Normal and 9.2 in RR', 'does not match 13/1 counts (r 0.05)', and the earlier impression of 'RR 8x higher' (a division artefact).
- Geography: blockage per 100 sessions by motor `country` (AU 19.7, CN 14.8, UK 7.2, KR 2.1, EU 1.1, US 0.75) and by region, each on 1 to 8 machines.
- Alarm source and severity emptiness: '686 of 972 SV50 blockage occurrences have no source', and the source/severity breakdowns (DDM, brush_bar_controller, fum_update_mgr and others).
- Monthly fault-rate tables and 'first seen month' tables used while looking at the trend (the trend itself is scripted in `key_numbers_v2.csv`, ids `ig_*`, but is not one of I1 to I4).
- Raw-CSV spot counts of SV50 and RR rows by model quoted in conversation (the scripted equivalents are `f_i2_ctx_*` and `f_i3_machines_*`).
- Numbers in `key_numbers_v2.csv` and the `n_` rows that are not copied to an `f_` row are scripted but not intended for the deck.
